03 / HOW WE WORK

From canopy scan
to final raking.

We operate like a surgical unit. Every project follows a rigorous safety-first protocol engineered over 37 years in the field.

01

Comprehensive Canopy Audit

Our certified arborist conducts a comprehensive ground and aerial inspection, identifying decay, weak crotches, and property proximity hazards.
