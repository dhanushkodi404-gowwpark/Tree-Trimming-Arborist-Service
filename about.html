Our Story • Est. 1987

Rooted in
expertise.
Grown with care.

Timber & Tonic was founded on a simple belief: healthy trees and safe properties go hand in hand. For over three decades, our family-owned crew has been caring for the landscapes that matter most to you.

We're not just tree cutters — we're certified arborists, stewards of the urban forest, and neighbors who care about the community we serve.

01 / OUR JOURNEY

From a single truck
to a trusted crew.

What started as one man, one truck, and a deep respect for trees has grown into the region's most trusted arborist service.

  • 1987 Jake Morrison founds Timber & Tonic with a single truck and an ISA certification.
  • 1998 Expanded to a full crew and added 24/7 emergency storm response.
  • 2012 Opened our permanent headquarters and added plant health care services.
  • 2026 37 years strong — over 12,000 trees cared for and counting.

When Jake Morrison started Timber & Tonic in 1987, he had one goal: to do tree work the right way. No shortcuts, no cutting corners — just honest, expert care delivered by people who genuinely love trees.

That philosophy hasn't changed. Today, our crew is made up of ISA Certified Arborists who bring decades of combined experience to every job. We've helped homeowners protect their properties from storm damage, cleared lots for new construction, and preserved the majestic old-growth trees that make our neighborhoods special.

We're proud to be a family-owned and operated business. When you call Timber & Tonic, you're not talking to a call center — you're talking to the same people who will show up at your door with saws in hand.

And we wouldn't have it any other way.

Jake Morrison

02 / WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our values.
Every cut.

These aren't just words on a wall. They're the principles that guide every job, every crew member, and every tree we touch.

01

Safety First

Every job begins with a hazard assessment. We follow ANSI standards, use proper rigging, and never compromise on the safety of our crew or your property.

02

Tree Health

We're arborists first and foremost. Our goal is always to preserve and enhance tree health — removal is a last resort, not a first option.

03

Honest Pricing

No hidden fees, no surprise charges. You get a detailed, transparent estimate before we start, and we stick to it.

04

Respect for Your Property

We treat your yard like our own. Cleanup is thorough, lawns are protected, and we leave your property looking better than we found it.
